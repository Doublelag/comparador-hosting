My verdict

Both set up a modpack in a few clicks and have a good network in Europe. The difference is the ceiling and the entry price. GPORTAL stops at 6 GB: not enough for a big modpack or a community. Godlike goes up to 33. On price GPORTAL wins the small plans: €11.80 for 4 GB against €9.74 at Godlike with the code, which lands close, except GPORTAL's −25% is first period only and Godlike's never expires. So: a small server and you can find your way around, GPORTAL; more than 6 GB or you want it made easy, Godlike.
