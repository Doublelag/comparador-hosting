My verdict

Both go after people who want the server to run smoothly, but they charge in opposite ways. DatHost has a single plan: 16 GB with a Ryzen 9 7950X and unlimited slots for €14.90 a month, no matter what. Godlike lets you build the plan — GB, slots and days —, runs a Ryzen one generation newer, up to the 9950X3D, and gives you up to three backups a day. The difference is the bill: their 16 GB is €50.99 a month at the normal rate (€40.58 paying the year), more than triple DatHost. So for 16 GB all year, DatHost. For a modpack of a few months, for smaller servers or if you want the fastest CPU, Godlike.
