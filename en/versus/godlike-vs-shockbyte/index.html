My verdict

Shockbyte is one of the best known and charges by RAM tiers: €19.26 for 8 GB with its discount already applied. Godlike is pricier at the normal rate, €25.99, but the first payment is €6.50 thanks to the biggest discount in the comparison. With 8 GB, the first two months cost €32.49 on Godlike and €38.52 on Shockbyte; by the third they're even. For a short-lived server, Godlike, with the bonus of paying only for the days you'll play. For one that runs for years, Shockbyte.
