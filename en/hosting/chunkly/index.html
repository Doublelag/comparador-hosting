The good

  • Among the cheapest in the table: €0.85 per GB on the Village range, 2 to 12 GB. 4 GB is €3.40 a month.
  • Price with VAT and in euros: what you see is what you pay, no surprise at checkout and no dollar conversion.
  • Free Campfire plan (2 GB, vanilla, about 3 players) to try the panel with no card.
  • Month to month, cancel any time. NVMe, DDoS protection, Java and Bedrock.

The catches

  • New brand: little track record and few reviews so far. The company behind it says 25 years in hosting, but Chunkly itself has only just started.
  • The cheap range (Village) only runs Paper, Purpur and Vanilla. For Forge, Fabric or a modpack you need Kingdom, which costs more than double: €1.90 per GB.
  • Capped at 12 GB on Village and 16 on Kingdom. They don't say which CPU they use.
  • Support in English only.

Verdict

On price it's one of the two or three cheapest in the table and, billing in euros with VAT, the price you see is the price you pay. It's new with little track record: try the free plan before moving your community over. For modpacks, Kingdom costs more than double and there you have real rivals.
