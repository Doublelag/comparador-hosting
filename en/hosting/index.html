The tricks you're going to run into

This site exists because this stuff made me angry. Not every host plays dirty, but the tricks that do get played are always the same ones, and almost none of them are illegal: it's fine print placed where you won't look.

  1. The headline price is only the first month. The most common one. You see "8 GB for €6.50", you pay, and next month it's €25.99. That's why on this site the price that counts is the normal rate, with the entry discount shown separately and named.
  2. The good price means paying two years upfront. Advertised huge, with a tiny "/mo". Cancel early and you get nothing back, and it renews at nearly double.
  3. "Unlimited slots" or "unlimited RAM". Unlimited doesn't exist. Behind it there's a CPU shared with twenty other servers, and you notice exactly when everyone logs in at once.
  4. The cheap range comes with no backups. You find out the day someone wrecks the world. It's in every review here.
  5. "Best hosting" rankings are written by the hosts themselves. They put themselves first, give themselves a 4.9 and save the real downsides for the competition. If a list doesn't say who pays for it, don't trust it.

Here prices are checked by hand on each host's own site, with the date shown, the table is sorted by what a gigabyte actually costs, and every review lists the downsides. I have no deal with any of them.
