The good

  • They have a data centre in Falkenstein (Germany): good ping from Spain.
  • On Performance and Performance+ the cores are dedicated, not shared with other customers.
  • Performance+ runs a Ryzen 9 9950X (7950X3D in Germany), among the fastest there is for Minecraft.
  • Their backup system lets you open a backup as a folder and restore a single file from the panel.
  • Every plan lets you create extra servers at no extra cost (Discord bots, websites, other games).

The catches

  • Essentials starts at 4 GB and uses shared cores with older CPUs (Ryzen 9 3900X outside Germany).
  • About $2.75 per GB on Performance and $3.50 on Performance+: mid-to-high pricing.
  • Their Performance+ coupon is for the first invoice only.
  • I couldn't find their refund policy or founding year on their site.

Verdict

Very good hardware and a data centre in Germany. Performance+ is among the best for a demanding server if you can afford it; for a small server with friends, there are cheaper options.
