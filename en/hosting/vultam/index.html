The good

  • They offer a free trial with no card required, and very few do that. You test it and you decide, without taking anyone's word for it.
  • Daily backups included on both lower ranges, not just the expensive one.
  • Nodes in France and Germany, which is what you want from Spain.

The catches

  • The Standard range runs an Intel i7-6700K, a 2015 processor. Fine for vanilla with four friends; you'll feel it with a modpack.
  • The Advanced range goes up to $2.40/GB, and at that price there's competition with newer hardware.
  • The Performance range, with a Ryzen 9 5950X and backups every 12 hours, costs $3.50/GB: at that point it barely competes with anyone.

Verdict

The free trial is the reason to look at them: they let you check it yourself before spending a euro. Don't expect miracles with mods on Standard; that processor has some years on it.
