The good

  • The price is simple arithmetic, no surprises: $3 per GB on Standard and $6 on Premium.
  • Java and Bedrock, one-click modpacks and plugins, and full FTP and MySQL access.
  • Around since 2013, with 24-hour chat support.

The catches

  • The Standard range runs DDR4 at 2133 MHz and a regular SSD: you'll feel it with a big modpack.
  • Premium, which does have NVMe and a fast CPU, costs double: $6 per GB, among the most expensive here.
  • Their welcome coupon is for the first invoice only.

Verdict

Standard is fine for vanilla or a few plugins, and for that price you can do better. Premium has good hardware, but at $6 per GB you'd have to really like them.
