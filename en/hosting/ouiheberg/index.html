The good

  • Cheapest per GB in the comparison: €0.50 per GB on the 4 GB plan (€1.99) and €0.62 on the 16 GB one (€10). Half of Chunkly and PebbleHost, the cheap ones until now.
  • Recurring price, VAT included, in euros: not a first-month promo and no dollar conversion. Month to month, no contract.
  • Marseille as a datacenter: of all the hosts compared, the closest to Spain. Frankfurt as a second European option.
  • Backups included (2 on Start, 5 on Pro), 2-4 vCPU at 3.7 GHz, NVMe, unlimited players and one-click modpacks from CurseForge and Modrinth.
  • They offer a free server for up to 4 players (Paper/Spigot, no mods) to try the panel with no card.

The catches

  • Only three sizes: 4, 8 and 16 GB. No 2 GB for four friends and no 12 GB for a mid modpack; above 16 you move to their OuiBox, which is a different product.
  • The 4 GB plan ships with 20 GB of disk: a big modpack plus its backups fills it. The 8 GB plan has 50 GB.
  • They don't publish the exact CPU model (“Ryzen infrastructure”, 3.7 GHz). Fine for vanilla and plugins; for a heavy modpack you want to know.
  • Ticket support in French and English. Spanish website, French team.
  • Their own “best Minecraft hosting” page ranks themselves first. It's not a comparison: it's their site.

Verdict

Today it's the cheapest per GB in the whole table and, on top, in euros, VAT included, with no first-month trick. If one of its three sizes (4, 8 or 16 GB) fits you and you're not a 30 GB-disk modpack, it's the first option I'd look at. If you need 2 GB or more than 16, it's not your place.
