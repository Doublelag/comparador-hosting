The good

  • A Spanish company, with its own panel and Spanish-language support.
  • Starts at €1 a month. To set something up and try it, it's next to nothing.
  • Ryzen 7 3700X and NVMe: solid hardware for what they charge.

The catches

  • Their biggest plan is 9 GB. For a heavy modpack or a big community it falls short and you'll have to move.
  • They don't publish where their data centres are, so I can't tell you what ping you'll get from Spain. I looked, and it isn't there.

Verdict

For a small, cheap server for friends, in Spanish, it's a very reasonable option. The day you need more than 9 GB, that's the end of the road.
