The good

  • They do Minecraft only, and you notice when you contact support: whoever answers knows what a modpack is.
  • Seven days to ask for a refund.
  • Paying the year it comes to $1.50/GB, which is competitive.

The catches

  • At the monthly price it's $3/GB: triple the cheapest in this table. 8 GB comes to about $24 a month.
  • To make it worthwhile you have to pay twelve months upfront, which is a bet before you know if you like the service.
  • The plans are tight: the 4 GB one comes with a single backup and 25 GB of disk.

Verdict

Paying month to month it's among the most expensive in the comparison, and nothing in the plan justifies it. It only makes sense if you pay the whole year, and even then I'd try another one on this list first.
