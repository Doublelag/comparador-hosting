The good

  • About $0.99 per GB: going toe to toe with PebbleHost Budget for cheapest, and here backups are included.
  • Five backups included and crash detection that alerts you.
  • You can switch games whenever you like without paying again.
  • A seven-day, no-questions refund.
  • Goes up to 124 GB, so there's room to grow.

The catches

  • Their smallest plan is 4 GB. If you want 1 or 2 GB for four friends, it isn't here.
  • They don't list the exact CPU model on the pricing page: you have to check their specs section.
  • A less-known brand than the big ones, so fewer outside reviews.

Verdict

On price it's among the best in the whole table, and unlike PebbleHost Budget the backups are included. If you need 4 GB or more, it's one of the first I'd look at; below that, it doesn't exist.
