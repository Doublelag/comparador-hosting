Hosting deals, with the fine print

Every coupon site tells you its deals are amazing. Here, only 3 of 8 strike me as genuine, and every one gets its fine print printed just as big.

Reviewed by hand on 20 September 2026

⚖️

The industry's favourite trick: advertising a huge discount that only lasts the first month, or that requires paying two years upfront. That's why every deal here comes with a verdict and the price you'll actually pay from month two.

HolyHosting Has a catch

25% off with a countdown

They advertise $0.95 per GB on the homepage, with a clock ticking down.

The fine print

The small print says it: 25% OFF on your first month. The real rate is $1.25/GB, so their 16 GB is $19.99 a month from the second month. That $0.95 you only pay once.

My advice: The countdown is there so you don't do the maths. Do it: multiply by 1.25 and compare with that.

The Minecraft Hosting I'd avoid it

60% off with the BLACKFRIDAY code

A 60% Black Friday code they say is “lifetime”.

CodeBLACKFRIDAY
The fine print

They call it Black Friday and it's running in September. A discount that never goes away isn't a discount: it's the price. Their 16 GB is listed at $35 so the 60% looks huge. And “lifetime” on a website isn't a contract.

My advice: If you're still interested, ask them in writing what it renews at before paying. There's too big a gap between $35 and $14 to trust one word on a banner.

WiseHosting A real deal

50% off paying yearly

Brings the GB down to $1.50 instead of $3.00 if you sign up for twelve months.

The fine print

It's a good old commitment discount. You pay the year upfront and that's it. No hidden trap. But it's twelve months in one go for something you haven't tried yet.

My advice: They have a seven-day refund. Sign up, really test it for those seven days, and if it convinces you, stay.

Shockbyte A real deal

30% off Minecraft, permanently

Their Minecraft discount is recurring, not first-month only. The price on their site already includes it.

The fine print

Careful when comparing: since it's already applied, the headline price IS the real price. Don't knock another 30% off in your head.

My advice: For Minecraft they charge per gigabyte. It helps if your plan is small; it doesn't save the big ones. 16 GB is still around $47.

Shockbyte A real deal

25% off paying yearly

A discount for signing up for 12 months. They also do 10% off paying quarterly.

The fine print

You pay the 12 months upfront. If the server doesn't win you over after a month and a half, the money's already gone.

My advice: Reasonable if you've already tried the service. Never as a first purchase.

Apex Hosting Has a catch

25% off with the APEX25 code

25% off your first order.

CodeAPEX25
The fine print

It's for the first month only. Their 16 GB goes from $53.99 the first month to $71.99 every month after. The discount hides that they're by far the most expensive in the comparison.

My advice: Don't let the 25% distract you: look at month two. For that $71.99 you get the same RAM for around €13.75 elsewhere.

Hostinger I'd avoid it

Up to 67% off

They advertise 16 GB at $13.99/month instead of $42.99.

The fine print

For that price you have to pay 24 months upfront ($335.76 in one go) and then it renews at $28.99/month, more than double the advertised price. The 67% is worked out from a list price almost nobody pays.

My advice: The most misleading deal I've found in this business. If you're still interested, do the maths at $28.99, which is what you'll actually pay.

Nodecraft Has a catch

30% off the first month and 24 hours free

They rebuilt their site in September: a 24-hour trial and the first month 30% cheaper.

The fine print

The −30% is for the first month only: 8 GB starts at $27.99 and renews at $39.98. And for the free trial they ask you to verify your phone, which they only tell you at the end.

My advice: The 24 free hours are good for seeing the panel. But at $4.99 per GB they're among the most expensive in the comparison: do the maths with month two's price.
