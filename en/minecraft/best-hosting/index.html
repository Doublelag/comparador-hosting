The three traps I found

Comparing hosting prices is hard on purpose. These are the three tricks I've seen over and over, and how to spot them:

  • The price that requires paying two years upfront. Hostinger advertises its 16 GB at $13.99 a month. The fine print says you have to pay 24 months in one go ($335) and that it then renews at $28.99. The real price is double the one in big letters.
  • The discount that only lasts the first month. Apex advertises 25% off. It's for the first month. Their 16 GB is $53.99 the first month and $71.99 every month after.
  • Selling “slots” without saying the RAM. If a host promises 20 or 50 players but doesn't publish the gigabytes, you can't know if your modpack will run. And usually it's because they'd rather you didn't know.

That's why the table above always uses the recurring price: what you'll pay in month 3, 6 and 12. Not the first-month hook.

The pricing model matters more than the brand

There are three ways they charge you, and they completely change who suits what:

  • Per gigabyte (Shockbyte, BisectHosting, PebbleHost, Apex). You pay for what you ask for, and there's a chasm between them: the same 16 GB goes from €13.76 at PebbleHost Budget to €61.91 at Apex.
  • Single plan (DatHost). One product: 16 GB, a Ryzen 9 7950X and unlimited slots. If you need 2 GB you overpay; from 12-16 GB it competes with the cheapest per gigabyte, with a better CPU.
  • By slots (Minecraft Realms). They charge by players and don't publish the RAM. Easy to understand, impossible to compare.

The practical takeaway: the “best host” depends on how much RAM you need, which is why this question has no single answer. Look at the table in your column, not overall.

Which one suits you

There's no “best host” full stop. There's a best host for your case, and it changes with how many of you there are and what you've installed:

If there are few of you and no mods

PebbleHost Budget

2-4 GB is plenty, and they charge $1 per GB: €3.44 for 4 GB. It's the cheapest there is. Two things before you sign up: the hardware is modest, which you won't notice with four friends in vanilla, and that range has no backups.

If the server will run all year

Kinetic Hosting or PebbleHost

Month to month, both are around €0.86 per GB: 16 GB for about €13.75. Kinetic includes five backups and starts at 4 GB; PebbleHost Budget has no backups.

If it's a seasonal server

Godlike.host

A modpack with friends, an event, a project of a few months. Godlike has the only discount in the comparison that never expires, −25% every month, and it's the only one that lets you pick the days (30, 90, 180 or 360). You can try it free for a day, and it runs latest-gen Ryzen with up to three backups a day. You pay for as long as the server lasts, not a month more. For a full year it costs more than the ones above.

If you need more than 16 GB

Kinetic, ScalaCube or the per-GB hosts

Single plans stop at 16 GB. Kinetic goes up to 124 GB (32 GB for about €27.50), ScalaCube to 64 and PebbleHost charges every gigabyte with no ceiling. Beyond that you're in dedicated-server territory.

Frequently asked questions

What's the cheapest Minecraft host?

Kinetic Hosting and PebbleHost Budget, at around €0.86 per GB every month; PebbleHost's Budget range has no backups. Godlike, even with its permanent −25%, sits at €2.44 per GB: there you are not paying for gigabytes, you are paying to have it made easy.

Is paying a year upfront worth it?

Only if you've already tried the server and know you'll stay. Take one month first, bring your people in and see how it goes. And be wary of anyone who only gives you the good price if you pay 24 months in one go.

What's the difference between paying per GB and a single plan?

Per GB you pay exactly for what you ask for, so it's cheaper if you need little and much more expensive if you need to grow. A single plan gives you plenty of RAM for a fixed price: you lose if you need 2 GB and win big from 8 up.

Are the prices on this page up to date?

I check them by hand on each host's website, and the date is under the table. Hosts change them without notice, so if you see something different, tell me and I'll fix it.
