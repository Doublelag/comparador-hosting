Cookie policy

Last updated: September 2026

This site sets no cookies. Not its own, not third-party, not analytics, not advertising. That's why you don't get a banner asking you to accept them.

What is stored, and why it doesn't need your consent

So the site remembers what you picked, a couple of things are kept in your own browser's storage. They never leave your device, can't be used to track you and aren't shared with anyone:

NameWhat forHow long
temaRemember whether you prefer night or light modeUntil you delete it
promo-vista-…Not showing you again a notice you've already closedUntil you delete it
cidKnow which network you came from (a YouTube video, for example)Deleted when you close the tab

These are technical and preference items you request yourself when using the site, so the law (article 22.2 of Spanish LSSI) doesn't require consent for them.

How to delete it

From your browser settings, under site data or storage. If you delete it, the site goes back to night mode.

If this changes

If something that does need your permission is ever added, this page will say so and you'll be asked first.
