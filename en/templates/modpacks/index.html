Why a modpack needs so much RAM

Every mod adds its blocks, items and recipes, and all of that sits in memory while the server is up. That's why a 500-mod pack with four friends needs more gigabytes than vanilla with thirty people. The numbers above come from the same maths as the calculator on the home page: a tier by player count, multiplied by how heavy the pack is.

And one warning that saves you money: more RAM doesn't make it faster. What actually moves a modpack is single-core CPU speed, because Minecraft runs almost everything on one thread. That's why the comparison lists each host's CPU.
