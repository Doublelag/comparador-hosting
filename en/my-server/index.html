Want to play on ChorizoSMP?

A Spanish-speaking semi-anarchy server. No base protection: stealing, raiding and fighting are allowed, but there's a real economy to get rich. Java and Bedrock in the same world.

Java (PC)chorizosmp.datho.st
Bedrock (mobile, console, Windows) chorizosmp.datho.st port 17095

Version 26.2 · Checking whether it's online…

What the server is like

🌶 Semi-anarchy

No claims and no base protection. Anything goes: steal, raid and destroy. There are still economy rules and an anticheat, though — this isn't 2b2t.

💰 Economy

Sell what you farm with /vender, buy spawners and more in /tienda, and bid in the auction house with /ah.

💎 Chorizium (CHR)

The second currency. You earn it in the AFK zones; in the PvP zone you earn five times more, but if you get killed, it's stolen. Spend it in /tiendachr.

⚔ No escaping combat

You can't flee a fight with commands, and if you disconnect mid-fight, you die.

🥩 You start from zero

You spawn with 8 steaks and 4 loaves of bread. No money, no tools, no armour. Leave spawn with /rtp and make your own way.

🎮 Crossplay and voice

PC, mobile and console players share the same world, and there's proximity voice chat with the Simple Voice Chat mod.

How to join

On PC (Java)

  1. Open Minecraft Java on version 26.2.
  2. Multiplayer → Add Server.
  3. Type chorizosmp.datho.st as the server address and hit Done.

On mobile or Windows (Bedrock)

  1. Play → Servers → Add Server.
  2. Address: chorizosmp.datho.st
  3. Port: 17095

On console

PlayStation, Xbox and Switch don't let you add external servers by hand. The easiest way is to join from a friend's phone or PC, or use one of the apps that let you join Bedrock servers from a console.
