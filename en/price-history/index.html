How to read this

  • The comparison is always on the 8 GB plan, the most common case, in euros.
  • A new host appearing in the comparison doesn't count as a change: me starting to track it doesn't mean they put prices up.
  • A few cents' difference on hosts that charge in dollars can come from the exchange rate, not from them. The rate used is printed in the tables.
