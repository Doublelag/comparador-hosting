Privacy policy

Last updated: September 2026

In short: this site doesn't ask you for any data, has no accounts or forms, uses no analytics and sets no cookies.

Controller

Doublelag. Contact: doublelag.manager@gmail.com.

What data is processed

  • Nothing you type. There's no sign-up, no forms and no comments.
  • Hosting technical data. The site is hosted on Netlify (Netlify, Inc., United States), which like any server logs technical data such as your IP address and browser to serve the page and protect it from attacks. Netlify is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
  • Fonts. The site's fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser requests those files from Google and Google receives your IP address. Google Fonts sets no cookies.
  • Server status. On the ChorizoSMP page, your browser asks api.mcsrvstat.us whether the server is online, which receives your IP to answer. Only on that page.
  • Visit measurement. I use Cloudflare's analytics, which sets no cookies and doesn't identify you: it counts page views and load times in aggregate. I don't know who you are and can't follow you around other sites, and there's no Google Analytics or anything like it.
  • Server templates. When you download a template, your browser fetches the plugins from their official site (api.modrinth.com, cdn.modrinth.com and download.geysermc.org), which receive your IP to send them. Only at that moment.

What's stored in your browser

A couple of preferences are stored in your own browser and never leave it: the night or light theme you pick, and whether you've closed a notice. See the cookie policy.

Links to hosts

When you click through to a host you leave this site and their privacy policy applies. Some links carry a tag saying which part of the site you came from, with no personal data.

Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability by writing to doublelag.manager@gmail.com. If you think something isn't right, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).
